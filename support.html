Support

Ways to get help using SQLite, report a problem, and support the people who maintain it.

Ask the community

The SQLite Forum (opens in a new tab) is the project's official, actively monitored discussion board — core developers read and answer questions there directly. It's the right first stop for "how do I…", "is this a bug", and design questions alike.

Report a bug

Post reproduction steps to the forum above rather than a separate tracker — that keeps the discussion and the fix in one place, and means a maintainer sees it quickly.

Commercial support and sponsorship

Companies that depend on SQLite in their own products can join the SQLite Consortium, which directly funds ongoing maintenance and testing infrastructure. Individual sponsorship is also welcome — see About for how the project is currently funded.
